I'm a software engineer who cares deeply about **design**, **craftsmanship**, and the long-term health of codebases. I spend most of my time working on backend systems and distributed infrastructure, but I'm equally comfortable in the frontend when the task demands it.

## What I Care About

- Writing code that is simple to understand and easy to change
- Building systems that survive contact with reality
- Sharing what I learn — both successes and failures

## Outside Work

When I'm not writing code, I'm usually reading about it. I keep this garden as a way to think out loud and maintain a living record of the ideas I find most useful.
